User Scenario Mapping

Scenario

Nora is a 22-year-old beauty enthusiast with an interest in skincare and makeup. She values products that enhance her natural glow while providing skin-loving benefits. As a college student, she seeks efficient and effective solutions that fit her busy lifestyle. Nora is passionate about clean beauty and often explores new trends and innovative products. She enjoys experimenting with different makeup looks, but her priority is always to maintain healthy skin. With an eye for detail and a desire to feel confident, Nora represents the modern consumer who appreciates quality, authenticity, and self-expression in her beauty routine.

Actor

Nora

Motivator

Wants to find makeup products that enhance her natural glow while also benefiting her skin.

Intention

Discover high-quality, skin-loving makeup products that promote a radiant complexion and provide skincare benefits.

Action

Nora browses the Velora website, exploring various product categories such as foundations, highlighters, and skincare-infused makeup. She uses the search bar to type in “glow” and applies filters for skin-loving ingredients like hyaluronic acid and vitamin C.

Resolution

Nora finds a highlighter and a foundation that match her needs, reads customer reviews, and decides to purchase them. She adds the products to her cart, completes the checkout process, and receives confirmation of her order along with skincare tips to maximize the benefits of her new products.
