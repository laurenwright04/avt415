Glossier

Brandy Melville

Brandy Melville is a trendy fashion brand known for its minimalist, West Coast-inspired aesthetic, offering casual basics like oversized sweaters and baby tees. However, its "one size fits most" policy has sparked controversy over size inclusivity.
